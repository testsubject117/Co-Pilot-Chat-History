## 3. `'Public Sub New()' in designer-generated type 'FormDeleteCheck' should call InitializeComponent method.`
That means your `FormDeleteCheck` constructor probably looks something like:

```vb
Public Sub New()
End Sub
```

but it needs:

```vb
Public Sub New()
    InitializeComponent()
End Sub
```

For WinForms, every form constructor must call `InitializeComponent()`.

---

## 4. `Access of shared member... through an instance; qualifying expression will not be evaluated.`
That means somewhere youâ€™re doing something like:

```vb

```

instead of referring to the shared member through the type itself.

Example:

```vb
Dim f As New FormAddOtherCheck()
f.SomeSharedValue
```

when `SomeSharedValue` is actually `Shared` and should be:

```vb
FormAddOtherCheck.SomeSharedValue
```

This is a warning/error about how a shared member is referenced.
