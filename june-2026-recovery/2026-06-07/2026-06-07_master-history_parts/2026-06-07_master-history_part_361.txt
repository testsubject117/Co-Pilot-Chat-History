## 1) Export all VMs (the real â€œVM backupâ€)

```powershell
# Export every VM
Get-VM | ForEach-Object {
    $vmDest = Join-Path $destRoot ($_.Name -replace '[\\/:*?"<>|]', '_')

    Export-VM -Name $_.Name -Path $vmDest

```

Notes:
- `Export-VM` captures the VM configuration, snapshots/checkpoints, and VHD/VHDX **as of export time**.
