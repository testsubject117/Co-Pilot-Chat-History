## Why those findings matter
These findings matter because they define the real **parity contract** that the WinForms application must either preserve or intentionally replace. The DOS system does not separate UI, business logic, and persistence cleanly. Instead:
- menu modules dispatch directly to business modules,
- business modules manipulate files directly,
- file schemas are implicit in `INPUT#` / `WRITE#` / random-record layouts,
- and data integrity is maintained partly through maintenance utilities and operator workflow rather than robust abstraction layers.

This means AMiWinForms modernization must be careful not to accidentally â€œmodernize awayâ€ critical legacy behavior. The safest current interpretation is:
- the data files and layouts are the system of record,
- the DOS workflows define expected behavior,
- and service/repository boundaries in VB need to be derived from those behaviors, not assumed from modern architectural preferences.
